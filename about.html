Our Story

North Star Bakery began in a small home kitchen, where our founder baked bread for friends and neighbors on Saturday mornings. As the line outside the door grew, so did the dream of a neighborhood bakery where everyone is welcome. Today we are still a small, family-run shop, and we still bake the way we did on that first Saturday: by hand, with patience, and with community in mind.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

Good bread starts with good ingredients. We buy flour from regional mills, eggs and dairy from nearby farms, and seasonal fruit from local growers whenever we can. Buying close to home keeps our baking fresh and supports the neighbors who support us.

  • Regional, stone-milled flour
  • Farm-fresh eggs and real butter
  • Seasonal fruit from local growers
  • No artificial preservatives

Meet Our Team

Marguerite Olsen, Head Baker

Marguerite has been baking for more than 20 years and shapes every Signature Loaf by hand. She loves teaching new bakers and sharing sourdough tips with customers.

Daniel Reyes, Pastry Chef

Daniel makes our croissants, danishes, and seasonal tarts. His cinnamon rolls are a neighborhood favorite on Sunday mornings.

Priya Shah, Cake Designer and Front of House

Priya works with families and event planners to design custom cakes, and she is usually the friendly face who greets you at the counter.
