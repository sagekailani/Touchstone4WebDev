Baked by Hand, Fresh Every Morning

Browse what we bake each day. Prices are approximate ranges, and there is no online checkout. Simply send us a pre-order request and we will confirm the details with you.

Breads

Our breads are mixed, shaped, and baked by hand each morning. Choose from sourdough boules, seeded multigrain loaves, baguettes, and soft sandwich loaves.

Price range: $5 – $9 per loaf

Pastries

Flaky, buttery, and made for sharing. Our pastry case includes croissants, fruit danishes, cinnamon rolls, scones, and cookies. Ask about dozen boxes for offices and family gatherings.

Price range: $3 – $6 each; $30 – $60 per dozen box

Cakes

From simple birthday cakes to tiered celebration cakes, we bake to order using real butter, cream, and seasonal fruit. Please allow at least 72 hours for custom cakes.

Price range: $35 – $120, depending on size and design
